Clinical Health Thresholds & Awaab's Law

< 12°C: Extreme hypothermia risk.
12°C - 16°C: Blood thickens, heart attack & stroke mortality spikes (Marmot Review).
< 16°C in poorly insulated homes: Triggers dew-point condensation and rapid mould spore development, creating actionable statutory non-compliance under Awaab's Law (Social Housing Regulation Act).

Thermal Equation Implementation:

Total Heat Loss Rate: L = FloorArea (m²) × HLP (W/m²K)

Continuous Power: P_avail = (Purchased kWh/wk × 1000) ÷ 168 hrs

Indoor Temp Achieved: T_achieved = T_ext + (P_avail + InternalGains [250W]) ÷ L

Cost of Delivered Heat: UnitRate (p/kWh) ÷ Efficiency or COP
